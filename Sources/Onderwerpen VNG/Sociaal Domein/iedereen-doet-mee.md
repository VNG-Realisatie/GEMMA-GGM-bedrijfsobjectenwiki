# Iedereen doet mee! | VNG

## Main Content

Since July 2016, the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities has been in effect in the Netherlands. Municipalities are required to develop a Local Inclusion Agenda.

### Core Principles

The initiative stands for:

- Taking "Nothing About Us Without Us" seriously by involving people with disabilities in all decisions
- Recognizing equal worth while valuing human differences
- Respecting individual choice -- participation means "as much as people can and want to"

### Key Resources

**Guidance Documents:**
- Handreiking Lokale Inclusie Agenda (available online and as PDF)
- Interactive map of Local Inclusion Agendas across Netherlands
- Self-assessment tool for municipalities implementing the UN Convention

**Learning Opportunities:**
- Digital knowledge sessions on accessibility, ableism, and inclusive participation
- Regional meetings across six Dutch regions
- Four-part masterclass series on implementing Local Inclusion Agendas

**Publications:**
- Inspiratiebundels (inspiration bundles) covering employment, housing, and play
- Handbooks on accessible voting, digital inclusion, and healthy environments
- Video/podcast content featuring collaboration between experts and civil servants

### Progress Monitoring

Flash surveys track implementation since 2018, with the 2025 survey available as a PDF resource.
