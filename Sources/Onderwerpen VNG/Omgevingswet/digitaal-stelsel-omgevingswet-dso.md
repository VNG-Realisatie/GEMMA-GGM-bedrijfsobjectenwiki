# Digitaal Stelsel Omgevingswet (DSO)

## Overview
The DSO is a digital system supporting the Environmental Act's implementation. It comprises local government systems and national provisions (DSO-LV), including the "Omgevingsloket" -- described as "a digital counter where initiators and stakeholders can quickly see what is permitted in the physical living environment."
